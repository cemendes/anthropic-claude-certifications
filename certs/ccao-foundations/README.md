# 🎓 Claude Certified Associate: Foundations (CCAO-F)

The official study track, scope breakdown, and high-yield preparation kit for the **Anthropic Claude Certified Associate: Foundations (CCAO-F)** exam.

---

## 📊 Exam Overview & Core Objectives

The **CCAO-F** validates foundational, day-to-day operational mastery of the Claude web and desktop platform, enterprise team workspaces, prompting techniques, and model ecosystem capabilities.

| Domain / Area | Exam Weight | Core Competencies |
|---|---|---|
| **Claude Ecosystem & Models** | ~25% | Strengths of Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Haiku, Opus; context windows (200k tokens), vision inputs, file formats. |
| **Artifacts & Interactive Outputs** | ~25% | Triggers for Artifact creation ($\ge 15$ lines of code, standalone HTML/SVG/React, markdown documents), modifying artifacts, version history. |
| **Projects & Knowledge Management** | ~25% | Project-level knowledge bases, custom system instructions, sharing projects with Team members, context boundaries. |
| **Prompting Foundations & Privacy** | ~25% | XML tag structuring (`<context>`, `<instructions>`), role framing, few-shot examples, zero data retention (ZDR), commercial privacy. |

---

## 🎯 Scoring & Format

* **Format**: ~45-60 Multiple-Choice Questions (Single & Multi-Select).
* **Time Limit**: 120 Minutes (Average completion time: ~60 minutes).
* **Style & Dynamics**: Concisely phrased questions with focused distractors; direct and fast-paced compared to architecture scenarios.
* **Delivery**: Online proctored or authorized test center.
* **Target Audience**: Business professionals, technical leads, Customer Engineers, and power users seeking a solid baseline in safe AI usage, core features, and prompting fundamentals.
